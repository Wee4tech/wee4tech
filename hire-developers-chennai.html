How does hiring developers through Wee4 Tech work?

You share the role and skills you need; Wee4 Tech Solutions shortlists pre-vetted developers from Chennai within 72 hours for common roles; you interview them; and the selected developer joins your team on a monthly contract, working on your projects under your direction. We handle payroll, compliance and replacement if a developer is not the right fit. Contract-to-hire and permanent placement are also available.

Hiring good developers is slow and expensive, and a wrong hire costs even more. Hiring through us lets you add skilled people in days, scale up or down month to month, and avoid the overhead of recruiting, payroll and notice periods.

Because we are a software company ourselves, candidates are assessed by practising engineers on real coding tasks — not just keyword-matched against a job description.

FAQ

Hire Developers — frequently asked questions

How quickly can a developer start?

For common skills such as .NET, React, Python and Flutter we usually share a shortlist within 72 hours, and the selected developer can typically start within a week. Senior or niche roles can take longer.

What is the minimum contract period?

Contracts are monthly. We agree the minimum term and notice period with you upfront, depending on the role.

Who manages the developer day to day?

You do — the developer works on your tasks, in your tools and meetings. We handle payroll, HR, equipment and replacement, and check in regularly on performance.

How is my code and IP protected?

Developers sign NDAs and IP-assignment agreements, and all work product belongs to you. Access to your systems stays under your control.

What is the difference between hiring developers and outsourcing a project?

When you hire developers, they work under your direction. When you outsource, you hand over a defined project and we manage delivery — see custom software development. For a full team managed by us, see our dedicated development team model.

I am a developer looking for a job. How do I apply?

Please submit your profile on our careers page. Our HR team will contact you when a suitable opening is available.
