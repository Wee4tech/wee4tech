Careers FAQ

How do I apply for a job at Wee4 Tech Solutions?

Fill in the application form on this page and upload your resume, or email it to hr@wee4techsolutions.com. Our HR team will contact you if your profile matches a current or upcoming opening.

Which locations do you hire for?

We hire for Chennai and for client positions in Coimbatore, Bangalore, Hyderabad and Pune.

Do you hire freshers?

Yes, we consider freshers with strong fundamentals. Our training courses can also help you build job-ready skills.
