What does an e-commerce development company do?

An e-commerce development company designs, builds and launches online stores, and connects them to payment gateways, shipping partners, inventory and accounting systems. Wee4 Tech Solutions builds Shopify, WooCommerce and custom e-commerce websites for retail brands, D2C businesses and distributors in Chennai and across India, including B2B dealer ordering portals.

Most online stores lose sales in the same places: slow product pages on mobile, a checkout that asks for too much, stock that does not match the shop floor, and orders that someone has to re-type into billing. We fix those first.

We pick the platform that fits your business — Shopify for speed to market, WooCommerce for flexibility on a budget, or a custom build when you need dealer pricing, complex catalogues or deep integrations — and we stay on after launch to keep the store fast, secure and selling.

FAQ

E-commerce Development — frequently asked questions

How much does an e-commerce website cost in India?

It depends mainly on the platform, the number of products and custom features, and the integrations you need (payments, shipping, billing, marketplaces). A Shopify or WooCommerce store costs less than a custom build, but has ongoing platform or plugin fees. Send us your requirements and we will share a fixed, itemised quote within 24 hours.

Should I choose Shopify, WooCommerce or a custom store?

Choose Shopify to launch fast with minimal maintenance, WooCommerce if you want flexibility and lower monthly fees and are comfortable with WordPress, and a custom build if you need B2B dealer pricing, complex product configuration or deep integration with your own systems. We recommend one after understanding your catalogue, volume and budget.

How long does it take to build an online store?

A Shopify or WooCommerce store usually takes 3–6 weeks from content sign-off. Custom and B2B e-commerce platforms typically take 8–14 weeks and are delivered in phases.

Can you move my existing store to a new platform?

Yes. We migrate products, customers and order history, keep your URLs or set up 301 redirects, and check Google Search Console after launch so you do not lose rankings.

Which payment gateways and shipping partners do you integrate?

Commonly Razorpay, PayU, UPI and cash on delivery for payments, and Shiprocket or direct courier APIs for shipping. We can integrate other gateways and logistics partners your business already uses.

Will I be able to manage products and orders myself?

Yes. You get an easy admin panel to add products, update prices and stock, and process orders, plus a training session and a short guide for your team.

Can the store sync with Amazon, Flipkart or my billing software?

Yes, using platform connectors or custom APIs we can sync products, stock and orders with marketplaces and with billing software such as Tally or Zoho.
