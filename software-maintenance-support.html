What is software maintenance and support?

Software maintenance and support is the ongoing work of fixing defects, applying security and framework updates, monitoring uptime, improving performance and adding small features after an application goes live. Wee4 Tech Solutions provides monthly maintenance plans for websites, web apps, mobile apps and business software — including systems built by other vendors.

Unmaintained software decays: libraries go out of date, security holes appear, integrations break when third-party APIs change, and small bugs pile up into lost revenue.

Our maintenance plans give you a dedicated team that knows your system, responds to issues quickly and plans improvements with you every month.

Who this is for

  • Companies with live apps but no in-house IT team

FAQ

Software Maintenance & Support — frequently asked questions

What does a software maintenance plan include?

Typically bug fixes, security and dependency updates, backups, uptime and error monitoring, performance checks and a monthly allocation of hours for small enhancements. We tailor the plan to how critical the system is to your business.

Can you maintain software that another company built?

Yes. We start with a short code and infrastructure audit, document what we find, fix urgent risks and then move to a regular maintenance cycle.

What are the four types of software maintenance?

Corrective (fixing bugs), adaptive (updating for new platforms, browsers or regulations), perfective (improving performance and usability) and preventive (refactoring and updates that stop future problems). Our plans cover all four.

How quickly do you respond to critical issues?

Response times are agreed in your plan. Production-down issues are prioritised over all other work, and you get a direct contact rather than a ticket queue.
