What is IT staffing and staff augmentation?

IT staffing is the recruitment of technology professionals for a company’s projects, either temporarily or permanently. Staff augmentation adds skilled people to your existing team for as long as you need them. Wee4 Tech Solutions sources, screens and deploys IT talent for businesses across India, with flexible contract, contract-to-hire, permanent and dedicated-team models.

Hiring good technical people is slow and expensive, and a wrong hire costs even more. Our Staffing-as-a-Service model lets you adjust your workforce quickly as demand changes, without compromising on quality.

Because we are an engineering company ourselves, candidates are assessed by practising developers — not just keyword-matched against a job description.

FAQ

IT Staffing & Resourcing — frequently asked questions

What is the difference between IT staffing and outsourcing?

With staffing or staff augmentation, professionals join and work under your team’s direction. With outsourcing, you hand over a defined project and the vendor manages delivery. We offer both — see custom software development for project-based work.

How quickly can you provide candidates?

For common skill sets we typically share a first shortlist within a few working days. Niche or senior roles can take longer.

Which engagement models do you offer?

Contract staffing, contract-to-hire, permanent placement and dedicated teams managed by Wee4 Tech.

I am a job seeker. How do I apply?

Please submit your profile on our careers page. Our HR team will contact you when a suitable opening is available.
