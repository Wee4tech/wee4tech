What is a dedicated development team?

A dedicated development team is a group of software professionals — developers, testers and often a project lead — who work full-time and exclusively on one client’s product, usually on a monthly contract. Wee4 Tech Solutions provides dedicated teams from Chennai, India for companies in India, the UK, the Middle East, Europe and North America, handling hiring, payroll, infrastructure and replacement while you direct the work.

Hiring a full engineering team locally is expensive and slow. Freelancers are hard to manage and rarely stay. A dedicated team gives you the stability of in-house staff at offshore cost, with the flexibility to scale month to month.

Your team members work only on your product, join your stand-ups, and use your Jira, GitHub and Slack or Teams. We take care of recruitment, HR, equipment and continuity.

FAQ

Dedicated Development Team — frequently asked questions

How much does a dedicated development team in India cost?

Cost depends on team size, seniority and skills. Pricing is a fixed monthly fee per team member with no hidden recruitment or infrastructure charges. Tell us the roles you need and we will send a team plan with monthly pricing.

How long does it take to set up the team?

Individual developers can usually start within one to two weeks. A full team with a project lead typically takes two to four weeks, depending on roles.

How do you handle time-zone differences?

We agree fixed overlap hours for stand-ups and reviews — for example, afternoons in India overlap with mornings in the UK and Europe, and the Middle East is only one to two hours apart. For US teams we arrange early-morning or evening overlap.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do. All team members sign NDAs and IP-assignment agreements, and all code lives in repositories you control.

Can we scale the team up or down?

Yes. Contracts are monthly, so you can add or release team members with the notice period agreed in your contract.
